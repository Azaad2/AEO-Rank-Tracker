# Outcome-led daily emails

## Goal
Change the existing daily newsletter from explaining product features to helping readers pursue clear business outcomes.

## What will change
- Replace the feature rotation with an outcome rotation: earn the first AI mention, close a competitor gap, become a cited source, win a buyer prompt, recover lost visibility, and turn visibility into qualified visits or leads.
- Write each email as a short growth brief: desired outcome, evidence to inspect, one practical action, an honest expectation, and a direct link to the relevant dashboard area.
- Use credible language only. Do not promise a #1 rank, 500 daily visitors, revenue, or a 30-day result unless verified customer evidence exists.
- Frame BndBox, Puubra, and “500+ brands” only as proof when supported by stored, attributable data; otherwise omit those claims.
- Update email labels, subjects, fallback copy, and call-to-action wording so the reader sees the result they can pursue rather than the feature being promoted.

## Technical details
- Update the daily newsletter function’s topic data and AI writing instructions.
- Keep the current audience, unsubscribe handling, 9 AM EST schedule, authentication, idempotency, and delivery provider unchanged.
- Deploy the updated function and verify it accepts the scheduled invocation without exposing or inventing customer data.
